The Evolution of Antiviral Defense Systems

A synthesis arguing that antiviral defenses across the three domains of life reuse a small set of designs, antisense recognition joined to nuclease activity and later to transcriptional and secreted responses, and proposing that chordates lost RNA interference through incompatibility with interferon.
