Benjamin tenOever & the tenOever laboratory

Viruses, host defense, and the biology between them.

Research on how cells detect viruses and how strongly they answer, and on rewriting viruses into instruments that ask questions no other method can. Across influenza, SARS-CoV-2 and other RNA viruses, the recurring subject is proportion, meaning which parts of a defense engage, how fast, and at what cost.
