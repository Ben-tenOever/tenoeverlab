The Spike D614G mutation increases SARS-CoV-2 infection of multiple human cell types

Isolating the SARS-CoV-2 Spike D614G substitution from the ORF1b P314L variant it travels with, through pseudotyped lentiviral particles and a trans-complementation assay with replication-competent virus, shows that the single Spike change raises entry efficiency across human lung, liver and colon cell lines without altering S1 affinity for ACE2.
