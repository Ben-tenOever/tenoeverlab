Non-cell-autonomous disruption of nuclear architecture as a potential cause of COVID-19-induced anosmia

SARS-CoV-2 infection of the olfactory epithelium reorganizes the nuclear architecture of uninfected olfactory sensory neurons, dissipating the interchromosomal compartments that hold olfactory receptor genes and suppressing receptor and signal transduction transcription in both hamsters and human autopsy tissue.
