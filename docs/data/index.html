Data catalog

Open scholarly data

Structured companions to the human-readable pages. Identifiers and relationships retain their provenance in the reviewed corpus.

  • publications.json — Publication metadata, full narrative sections, attribution, identifiers, and relationships
  • discoveries.json — Discovery statements, full narratives, and evidence-to-publication mappings
  • research-areas.json — Six research areas with themes, publications, and discoveries
  • themes.json — Research area and theme membership
  • connections.json — 129 documented directed publication relationships
  • claims.json — Original claim-to-source mappings
  • vocabulary.json — Controlled terms and publication memberships
  • timeline.json — Chronology and scientific transitions
  • publication_inventory.json — Source inventory, including an inventory-only entry; the 63 reviewed records define site coverage
  • search_index.json — Original search index
  • provenance.json — Source checksums and scope

Conventions

Publication IDs are stable slugs. Discovery IDs follow the original claim IDs. Publication canonical_url fields are absolute; original url fields are site-relative. Research-area URLs are absolute. Graph from/to fields reference publication IDs; substantiated_by references supporting papers. Missing identifiers are null, not inferred.

tenOever Laboratory

A source-grounded account of a research program. The reviewed collection contains 63 publications from 2003–2025; it is not a complete or continuously updated bibliography. Attribution distinguishes lab-led, co-led, collaborative, and training-period work.
