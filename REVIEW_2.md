# Knowledge Base Review

Everything below was built from the 63 publications in your Dropbox folder, read in full. Nothing here is drawn from abstracts, and no identifier was written that was not first verified against Crossref and NCBI. This is the review gate. The website is not built yet, by design, because a taxonomy or an attribution decision you disagree with is cheap to change now and expensive once it is baked into eighty static pages.

## How attribution came out

Applying your rule of judging by the contributions statement rather than by authorship position alone.

- **lab-led** 37
- **co-led** 10
- **collaborative** 14
- **training period** 2

The two training-period papers are Sharma 2003 from the Hiscott laboratory and tenOever 2007 from the Maniatis laboratory. Both are presented honestly as predating the independent laboratory, and the intellectual history uses them to show where the interferon signaling thread actually starts.

Every synthesis document names the leading laboratory wherever work was led elsewhere. The key discoveries document declines to claim the capicua repressor finding and the stress granule finding for this program, crediting the Manicassamy and Hur laboratories respectively, and it omits the ancient DNA paper entirely on the grounds that its record states the connection to the corpus runs through personnel rather than science.

## Two places the record shows the program revising itself

Both are treated as strengths rather than smoothed away, and both became structural to the trajectory document.

The first is the Drosha revision. Shapiro 2010 reported cytoplasmic microRNA processing as microprocessor independent. Shapiro 2012, using cleaner genetics, found Drosha absolutely required and said so. The correction is more consequential than the original claim, because it drew attention to Drosha leaving the nucleus during infection, which produced Shapiro 2014 and Aguado 2017 and ended in a position neither side of the 2013 RNAi dispute had held.

The second is the interferon dissemination question. Hoagland 2021 proposed, as speculation, that disseminated viral RNA explained antiviral activity in tissues SARS-CoV-2 barely reaches. Carrau 2023 from the same laboratory tested it and reported instead for airway-derived circulating interferon priming distal organs.

## Where the corpus says lines of work stopped

An honest program history includes these, and the synthesis names them rather than implying continuity.

- The small viral RNA line ran from 2010 to 2012 and stopped. How those RNAs are generated is still unknown.
- The RNA delivery vector reached mouse airway and no paper in the corpus shows it silencing a target in an animal.
- Lineage tracing of surviving infected cells produced one paper and was developed further by other laboratories.
- The recipient-side barrier that reduces an airborne influenza inoculum to as few as two founder genomes was never characterized.
- The structural questions left open by Ng 2011 and Schmid 2014 were not returned to.
- No viral antagonist explaining the low interferon phenotype of SARS-CoV-2 was identified anywhere in the corpus.

## The relationship graph

129 relationships between publications, every one of them stated in a paper's own reference list or in its record rather than inferred from topic similarity. 58 of the 63 publications are connected to at least one other. The five that are not are Møller 2018, Muñoz-Moreno 2019, McCune 2020, Si 2021 and Paget 2023, four of which are collaborative work led by other groups.

Where two papers share a first author and a year and cannot be told apart from the citing text, no edge was created rather than guessing. That affects the two Nilsson-Payant 2021 papers, the two Benitez 2015 papers, the two Langlois 2012 papers and the two Daniloski 2021 papers.
